What is the $299 window-tint offer?

Get Your Vehicle Tinted only for $299 (Includes all 4 windows and Rear Windshield). The front windshield is not listed as included. Confirm vehicle eligibility, film, shade, removal charges and taxes with Tamiami before booking.

Is the $299 offer carbon or ceramic tint?

Tamiami’s website lists full sides and rear carbon tint starting at $299, ceramic starting at $399 and premium ceramic starting at $499. Confirm the film and exact terms for this promotional offer directly with the shop; ceramic is not promised here.

How long does window-tint installation take?

Timing depends on your vehicle, glass coverage, film and any existing-tint removal. Call Tamiami with your year, make and model for an estimate and appointment availability.

How do I care for my new tint?

Tamiami recommends keeping windows up for 3–5 days, longer in humid weather, and waiting 1–2 weeks before cleaning the inside glass. Use a soft microfiber cloth and ammonia-free cleaner. Follow the instructions given for your installation.

What shade and warranty come with the offer?

Shade options and warranty coverage depend on the film and your vehicle. Ask for the specifications and written warranty terms before booking, and choose a configuration that meets current Florida requirements.
